**The question**
If you had to summarise what he was on about, what would it be?

**The text**
Mark 1:14–15; Matthew 5:3–10 next to Luke 6:20–26.

**What we will look at**
"kingdom" as political and hopeful language, not a place you go when you die; already and not-yet; why Matthew's "poor in spirit" and Luke's blunt "you who are poor" both exist and what the difference tells us about how the Gospels were shaped.

The examen. Walk back through your day; name one moment you're grateful for and one you'd redo.
