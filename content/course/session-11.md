**The question**
Do I have to have it all resolved first?

**The text**
Mark 9:24; Matthew 28:16–17 ("but some doubted"); John 20:24–29 (Thomas).

**What we will look at**
Doubt inside the texts themselves, not smoothed over; *pistis* (faith) as trust and allegiance, closer to loyalty than to certainty.

**Try this**
Write out your live doubts, plainly and specifically. Not to resolve them. To see them.

**One next step**
Bring one doubt to the table next week.
