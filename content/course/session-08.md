**The question**
What actually got him executed?

**The text**
Mark 11:15–18 (the temple), then the trial in Mark 14:53–65 and 15:1–15.

**What we will look at**
Crucifixion as public Roman terror; the temple action as the likely trigger; the mix of political and religious charges; what each Gospel emphasises and why.

**Try this**
Honest prayer. Say one true sentence out loud to God, even if it's anger or "I don't know if you're there."
