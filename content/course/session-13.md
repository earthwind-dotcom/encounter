**The question**
Is this a solo thing, or not?

**The text**
Acts 2:42–47; 1 Corinthians 12:12–27.

**What we will look at**
The honest history, the church's failures next to its witnesses; "church" as a people, not a building or an institution; what community is actually for in this.

**Try this**
Come to one gathering of the wider church, as an observer, no expectations.
