**The question**
Why do Christians say a first-century execution matters for me?

**The text**
Mark 10:45; Romans 3:21–26; Isaiah 52:13–53:12.

**What we will look at**
The several images the New Testament uses (a ransom paid, a sacrifice made, a victory won, an example set) and that no single "theory" is the New Testament's own; how the language borrows from the temple and the marketplace.
