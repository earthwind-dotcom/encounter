**The question**
What would a daily rhythm even look like?

**The text**
Matthew 6:9–13 next to Luke 11:1–4 (compare the two versions of the Lord's Prayer).

**What we will look at**
What each petition meant in its setting; *lectio divina* and the examen as old, tested practices, not techniques; how to read a passage slowly (ties back to session 2).

**Try this**
Design a 15-minute daily rhythm you'll actually keep. Small enough to be boring.

**One next step**
Run your rhythm for a week and report what broke.
