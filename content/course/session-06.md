**The question**
Love your enemies. Turn the other cheek. Literal instruction, or ideal?

**The text**
Matthew 5:38–48.

**What we will look at**
The first-century legal and social setting of "the other cheek," "the second mile," "the cloak"; Walter Wink's reading of these as calculated, dignified resistance rather than passivity; where scholars push back on Wink.

**Try this**
Name one relationship where the reflex to hit back is live right now. Don't act. Just sit with it.

**One next step**
One week of not sending the sentence that would win and also wound.
