## A second reader for everything

Before anything else: every course session and every Hard Question gets a careful second read,
with every fact and source checked. When one has been, it'll say so.

## More hard questions

The next ones, some of which need pastoral care as well as scholarship before they're published:
why a loving God would need a sacrifice; whether Christianity is anti-women; sexuality and
gender; the Crusades, colonialism and slavery; the Holy Spirit; evil.

## The course, in Spanish

Hard Questions are already in Spanish. The thirteen sessions come next, then Portuguese.

## Running it with a group

Groups exist now. Next: printable one-page handouts for each session, a simple way for a
facilitator to schedule the weeks, and training for people who want to lead a table.

## Audio and video

A short talk for each session and each Hard Question, for people who'd rather listen than read.

## Wonder

The same honesty, built for children, so that nothing they're told has to be unlearned at
nineteen. It won't be written until a proper review of what already exists has been done and the
real gaps are known.

## A home of its own

A proper web address, and email, so you can get a password reset without asking a person and
hear when something new is published, if you want to.
