## Finished

- **Hard Questions.** Eighteen answers, in English and Spanish.
- **The Library.** Fifteen sermons (Reflections), five word studies (Roots) and ten studies of
  where texts came from (Provenance), carried over from Marginalia.
- **The Course.** All thirteen sessions have a participant guide and a facilitator guide.
- **Accounts, groups and Talk.** Optional accounts that save your place and private notes; groups
  for running the course together; a way to reach a real person.

## Drafts, and what that means

- **Every course session is a draft.** They're written and usable, but none has had a second
  reader check every fact and source yet. Each session page says so.
- **No Hard Question has been reviewed by a second person yet.** They follow the rules below, and
  every book cited is a real, published book. A second careful read is still owed.

## In progress

- **Translation.** The interface is in English, Spanish and Portuguese. Hard Questions are in
  English and Spanish. The course and most of the Library are English only, and each page says so
  when you switch language rather than quietly showing you the wrong thing.
- **Portuguese** hasn't been read by a native speaker. Treat it as a working draft.

## Not started

- **Wonder**, the children's approach. The approach, three age bands and one worked sample are
  written. The curriculum isn't, and it shouldn't be built until a proper review of existing
  children's material has been done.
- **Email.** There's no email from Encounter yet, so a forgotten password is reset by asking a
  person through Talk.

## Corrections made

- **28 September 2026.** Marginalia became Encounter. Every article kept its text, translations
  and sources; links to the old single page now go to real addresses.
- **7 September 2026.** The confidence labels were being used with eight different names against a
  legend that defined three. They're now five, defined once, and applied everywhere.
- **7 September 2026.** Twelve Reflections were published without the line that says where each
  claim sits. They have it now.
- **7 September 2026.** A claim about how many times the King James Version renders *Sheol* as
  "hell" is quoted everywhere as thirty-one. It isn't printed here, because it hasn't been checked
  against a concordance.
